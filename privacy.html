Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This page explains what information Mayorcity E-Mart collects, why, and how it's protected.

1. Information we collect

When you create an account, we collect your full name, email address, phone/WhatsApp number, matric number, department, level, and a photo of your student ID card. When you post a listing or a Lost & Found report, we collect the details you provide (item name, description, price, photos, contact info). When you pay a listing fee, Paystack processes your payment — we never see or store your card details ourselves; we only receive a payment reference confirming the transaction succeeded.

2. How we use your information

Your registration details are used to verify that you're a genuine student before allowing you to post listings. Your listing information is shown publicly on the marketplace so other students can browse and contact you. Your student ID photo is visible only to you and to platform admins/moderators reviewing verification requests — it is never shown publicly.

3. Where your data is stored

Your data is stored with Supabase, our database and authentication provider, using industry-standard access controls (Row Level Security) so that sensitive fields like your student ID and phone number are only accessible to you and authorized admins — never to other regular users or the public. Payments are processed by Paystack under their own security and compliance standards.

4. Who can see what

  • Your seller name and listing details — visible to anyone browsing the marketplace.
  • Your WhatsApp number — shared only when you choose to post a listing that includes it, since that's how buyers contact you.
  • Your student ID photo, matric number, department, and level — visible only to you and to admins/moderators reviewing verification.
  • Your email — never shown publicly; used only for login and account-related communication.

5. Data retention and deletion

We retain your account and listing data for as long as your account is active. If you'd like your account and associated data deleted, contact us via the Contact section and we'll process the request.

6. Cookies and local storage

The platform uses your browser's local session storage to keep you signed in between visits. We don't use tracking cookies or sell your data to advertisers.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the platform grows. Continued use of the platform after changes are posted means you accept the updated policy.

8. Contact

Questions about your data or this policy? Reach out via the Contact section on our homepage.
